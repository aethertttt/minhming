My dearest,

I have started this letter more times than I can count, and every time the words feel too small for what I want to say. So I will begin with the simplest thing: you make my days softer.

It is in the little moments that I find you most. The way you laugh before you finish the joke. The quiet of a morning with you in it. The way home stopped being a place and became a person.

Thank you for your patience on the days I was hard to love, and for staying through the ordinary ones. I think that is where love really lives.

Whatever comes next, I want to meet it beside you.

Always yours, Jae
